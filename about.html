ABOUT OUR CLUB

A community united by cricket.

Club History

Dungsree Paschim Para Cricket Club is a cricket community built on teamwork, discipline, friendship and a love for the game. Established in 2026, DPPCC aims to create opportunities for players and bring our community together through cricket.

Our Mission

To encourage participation, develop cricketing skills and build a respectful, united team.

Our Vision

To become a respected community cricket club known for sportsmanship, teamwork and positive impact.

Established 2026

Our club began in 2026. Sponsor branding references 2003 separately and does not represent the club's founding year.
