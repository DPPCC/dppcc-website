ABOUT DPPCC

Our history, mission and vision

Club History

Dungsree Paschim Para Cricket Club (DPPCC) is a community cricket club built on teamwork, discipline, friendship and a love for the game.

Established 2026

DPPCC was established in 2026 to bring players and the local community together through cricket.

Our Mission

To encourage participation in cricket, develop players' skills and create a respectful, inclusive sporting environment.

Our Vision

To grow as a respected community cricket club known for sportsmanship, teamwork and positive contribution.

Our Values

Unity, discipline, respect, fair play and continuous improvement.
